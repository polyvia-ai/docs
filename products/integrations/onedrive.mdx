The same picker covers personal OneDrive and SharePoint sites your account has access to. No setup required in Polyvia.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Import from OneDrive">
    On the Documents page, click the **Import from OneDrive** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Microsoft">
    The Microsoft picker opens. Sign in with the account that has access to the files (personal OneDrive or a SharePoint site).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick one or more files">
    Browse OneDrive folders or SharePoint document libraries and select files.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching account

The picker uses your Microsoft session cookie. To use a different account, open **Settings → Integrations** in Polyvia and click **Switch account** on the OneDrive row — this signs you out at Microsoft. The next click on Import will prompt you to sign in again.
